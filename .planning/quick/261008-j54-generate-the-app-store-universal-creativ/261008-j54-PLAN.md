<objective>
App Store Connect's redesigned product page has two OPTIONAL creative slots,
Header and Search Results. One 16:9 master at 5244x2950 is valid in both, and
Apple recommends exactly that for brand recognition. Build the generator for
it, in the same idiom as the Play feature graphic, so the asset is reproducible
and the mark is never drawn twice.

Specs confirmed against Apple's own page
(developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/
creative-assets-specifications/): header 21:9 3840x1646 or 16:9 5244x2950;
search results 3:2 1920x1280..3840x2560 or 16:9 5244x2950; .png/.jpeg/.jpg;
**alpha not allowed**.
</objective>
