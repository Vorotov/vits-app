<objective>
Close the measured bilingual render gap. Criterion 1 is a claim about SCREENS, so the proof must render screens — and today five of them have never been rendered in English by any test.

Purpose: the ARB audit proves the strings are there; it does not prove they reach the screen, that the screen survives them, or that the screen is even in the right language. This plan converts criterion 1 from an audit result into a rendered, per-screen assertion, and proves the criterion-3 propagation claim against the two surfaces that could plausibly break it — a pushed route and an open modal sheet.

Output: a shared bilingual-assertion helper and a locale × text-scale matrix across four existing test files, adding zero production code.
</objective>
