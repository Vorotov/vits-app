<objective>
Replace the Stack screen's full-width add button with a single floating add affordance mounted once on the shell, so "exactly the three tabs and nowhere else" is a structural fact rather than a flag anyone can get wrong.

Purpose: UX-01. Two affordances for one action is worse than either alone; the v1 spec's explicit rejection of a FAB (02-UI-SPEC D6) is reversed by the approved v1.1 spec §4, and its stated reason — a duplicate affordance on a 390pt screen — is satisfied by deleting the *button*, not the FAB.
Output: `BqAddFab`, its theme entry, its mount point, the rewritten Stack empty-state copy, and presence/absence tests.
</objective>

## Artifacts this plan produces

- `lib/core/widgets/bq_add_fab.dart` — a thin, semantics-correct wrapper over the SDK floating action button.
- A `floatingActionButtonTheme` entry in `bqTheme()` — the only place the FAB's colours are written.
- `test/core/widgets/bq_add_fab_test.dart` — semantics activation, sheet opening, double-tap guard.
- A rewritten `emptyStackBody` in both locales, repointed at the + action.

## Artifacts this plan removes

- The full-width `SizedBox(width: double.infinity, child: FilledButton(addSupplement))` from `stack_screen.dart:82-101` and its trailing `SizedBox(height: BqSpace.md)` at `:102`.
- Three doc comments that become false the moment the button goes: `stack_screen.dart:7-8` and `:181-182` (both describing the empty state as sitting under a still-visible CTA), and the empty-day comment that moved into `today_screen.dart` (formerly `calendar_screen.dart:479-480`, "named, not linked, because the nav bar is the affordance").
- The Stack screen's accent budget entry — after this plan the screen has no accent-filled block of its own.
- Nothing in `add_supplement_sheet.dart`. The sheet is untouched.

Add a `floatingActionButtonTheme` to `bqTheme()`: background from the accent token, foreground from the surface token, `elevation`, `focusElevation`, `hoverElevation` and `highlightElevation` all 0, and the splash colour from the pressed-accent token. Record the reason for zero elevation in a comment: the design brief asks for "the app's existing elevation language", and this app *has none* — the v1 bar was flat and 04-UI-SPEC states there is no Material elevation anywhere on the planner screens. A flat accent disc on the paper background has ample contrast without a shadow, and a shadow here would be the first one in the app.

Create `lib/core/widgets/bq_add_fab.dart` holding a `Semantics(button: true, label: <the add-supplement ARB label>, excludeSemantics: true, onTap: open)` wrapping the SDK floating action button with `onPressed: open` and the add glyph as its only child. **Both** the wrapper's `onTap` and the button's `onPressed` call the same opener — `excludeSemantics: true` drops every descendant action, and this is the same WR-02 rule the nav bar follows. No `tooltip:` — it would be dropped by the semantics boundary anyway, and this app renders no tooltips. No colours in this file; they come from the theme.

`open` calls `showAddSupplementSheet(context)` unchanged. Before writing the double-tap test, **read the sheet's own guard** rather than assuming one exists; if it does not guard, the guard belongs in this widget and the summary must say so.

Mount it once: `floatingActionButton: const BqAddFab()` on `AppShell`'s **root** `Scaffold`. Do not add it to any screen's inner `Scaffold` — those sit inside the shell's body and a FAB there would render under the bar. Do not introduce a per-screen visibility flag; the structural mount is what makes "exactly three tabs, nowhere else" true, and Settings, as a pushed route with its own `Scaffold`, can never inherit this one's FAB.

Leave the position as the `Scaffold` default end-float location. Do not write a clearance offset above the bar: the `Scaffold` measures the real bottom navigation bar, whose height is the nav bar's computed extent, so the clearance rises with the bar for free. A hand-positioned offset would reproduce the exact defect class this phase exists to remove.

Add the theme assertions to `theme_test.dart` and the behaviour tests to a new `test/core/widgets/bq_add_fab_test.dart`.
