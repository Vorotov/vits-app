<objective>
Turn the shell into the three tabs v1.1 actually wants — Стек, Сьогодні, Календар — by promoting the Today page into its own screen, deleting the Calendar screen and the whole in-tab page-swap mechanism, and removing Settings from the bar now that a gear reaches it from everywhere.

This is the phase's deletion-heaviest plan. It is sequenced so the codebase compiles and the suite is green at every commit: Task 1 is a behaviour-preserving extraction that leaves the page swap intact, Task 2 performs the restructure and retargets every consumer in one atomic commit, Task 3 adds the absence gates that keep the deleted machinery from creeping back.

Purpose: NAV-02, and the second half of NAV-03.
Output: `today_screen.dart`, a three-destination shell, and source-glob gates proving the page swap is gone.
</objective>
