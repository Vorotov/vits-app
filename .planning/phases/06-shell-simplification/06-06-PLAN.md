<objective>
Finish PLAN-05: delete every judgement the planner layered on top of concurrent load — verdict chips, slot pips, the limit badge, the year footnote, the banded chip colours and the red month counts — along with the constants and the sixteen copy keys that existed only to express them, and tighten the vocabulary gate now that limit words are illegitimate everywhere in planner copy.

What remains is the useful half: which supplements overlap in a given week, and how many. That was always the answer; the verdict was the opinion layered on it.

Purpose: PLAN-05, completed and proven by absence.
Output: a planner with no coloured judgement anywhere, a rewritten disclaimer, sixteen fewer ARB keys, and gates that fail if any of it returns.
</objective>
