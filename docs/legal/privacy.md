# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

## Who we are and what this covers

VitoMy is a planner and tracker for the supplements and vitamins you
choose to take. It is developed and operated by an independent team of
individuals working from different countries (the "VitoMy team", "we",
"us"). The team is not yet incorporated as a company; the Terms of Use say
what happens if that changes.

This policy covers:

- the VitoMy mobile app on iOS and Android (the "App"); and
- the support address support@vitomy.app (together with the App, the
  "Services").

## The short version

Everything you enter in the App stays on your device. There is no account, no
server, and no copy of your data with us. The one thing the App sends anywhere
is a tip, if you choose to leave one, and none of your entries go with it.
Reminders never include the name of what you take. Delete the App and its data
goes with it.

## What stays on your device

When you use the App, it stores on your device:

- the supplements you add, with the doses and times you set;
- your schedules, cycles and breaks;
- your intake log: which entries you marked as taken, and when;
- the language you chose, if you overrode the system language; and
- whether you have seen the intro and the one-time hints.

This data lives in the App's private storage, in a database file that only
the App can read. It never leaves your device: the App contains no code that
sends any of it anywhere, and the one connection the App makes, to offer and
confirm a tip, carries none of it. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it
for you.

Your device's backup may include the App's data. On iOS that is iCloud
Backup; on Android it is Google's backup service. Those backups are governed
by Apple's and Google's terms, not by this policy, and you control them in
your device settings.

## What we receive

Through the App: nothing, unless you choose to leave a tip. If you do, see
"If you leave a tip" below.

From Apple and Google: if you opted in to share analytics and crash reports
with app developers in your device settings, Apple and Google may show us
aggregated crash and usage statistics for the App. These come without your
name or contact details and are collected under Apple's and Google's own
terms.

From you, if you write to us: the address you write from and whatever you
put in the message. We use it only to answer you. Our legal basis is our
legitimate interest in replying to people who write to us. We keep the
message as long as it takes to resolve the question and any follow-up.

## If you leave a tip

The App has a screen where you can leave a one-off tip towards its
development. It unlocks nothing and changes nothing in the App.

Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card, your billing
address or your name. To offer the tip and to confirm it went through, the App
talks to RevenueCat, Inc., a purchase-management provider acting for us under
a data-processing agreement. RevenueCat receives the purchase and its receipt,
an identifier for your installation of the App, and the network address the
purchase came from, which it uses to work out the country.

RevenueCat receives none of your entries. It cannot: the part of the App that
talks to it has no way to reach your supplements, your schedules or your
intake log, and an automated check in our build process fails if that stops
being true.

The identifier is generated at random and is not derived from anything about
you or your device. It is not tied to your name, and we do not connect it to
anything else. Reinstalling the App produces a new one.

Our legal basis is performing the contract you enter into when you choose to
leave a tip.

## What we do not do

No ads, no advertising or tracking SDK, no analytics SDK, no data brokers, no
profiling. The one third-party component in the App handles purchases and does
nothing else. We do not sell, rent or share data. The App does not read or write Apple Health or
Health Connect, and it does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission,
because there is nothing to track.

## Permissions

The App asks for one permission: notifications, to deliver the reminders
you schedule. Reminders are created and shown on your device; no push
service is involved. You can turn the permission on or off at any time in
your device settings. Without it, reminders stop and everything else keeps
working.

On Android the App also declares that it may run after the device restarts,
so scheduled reminders survive a reboot. That declaration gives the App no
access to anything else.

## Legal framework and your rights

We design the Services to comply with EU and UK data-protection law (GDPR /
UK GDPR), with US state consumer-privacy laws where applicable (for example
California's CCPA/CPRA and comparable laws in other states), and with other
local privacy laws that apply where our users are located.

Because the App keeps your data on your device and we hold none of it, you
exercise most rights yourself, directly in the App: view and edit every
entry, delete one, or delete the App to remove everything it stores. Remove
the device backup too if you do not want a copy there. Your
data sits in the App's private storage on your device, and the App does not
yet have an export feature.

For the little we do hold (messages you send us), you can ask us to access,
correct, delete or restrict it, object to our use of it, or complain to a
data protection supervisory authority. Write to support@vitomy.app. To verify a
request we confirm that it comes from the address the original message came
from. We respond within the time your local law sets.

We do not sell personal information, for money or otherwise, and we do not
share it for advertising. If you are a resident of a US state with its own
privacy law, there is nothing to opt out of. If we decline a request you
made under your state's law, you may appeal by replying to our answer with
"PRIVACY APPEAL" in the subject line.

## Children

The App is for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not
knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent
us personal data, write to support@vitomy.app and we will delete it.

## Third-party services

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play, which
have their own privacy policies covering what they collect when you download
and update apps. Those stores are governed by their own policies, not this
one, and we are not responsible for them.

One service is built into the App itself: RevenueCat, Inc., which manages the
optional tip described above. It acts as our processor, it receives only what
that section lists, and it is bound by a data-processing agreement. It is the
only such service in the App, and if another is ever added, this policy and
the App's store listings change in the same release, before that version
ships.

## Security

Your data is protected by your device: the App's private storage, the app
sandbox, your device passcode, and encrypted backups where the operating
system provides them. We have no server holding your data. No system is
perfectly secure; keep your device updated and locked.

## International transfers

If you leave a tip, the purchase information described above reaches
RevenueCat, Inc. in the United States. Messages you send us are stored with
our email provider, which may also be outside your country. In both cases we
rely on the legal protections that applicable law provides for such
transfers, including the standard contractual clauses where they apply.
Nothing you enter in the App is transferred anywhere.

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. For changes that
matter, we also show a notice in the App, and where the law requires it we
ask for your consent. If we ever add a service that receives
data from the App, this policy and the App's store listings change in the
same release, before that version ships.
